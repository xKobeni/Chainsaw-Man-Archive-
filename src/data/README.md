# Volume metadata sources

The previous prototype generated Part 1 chapter ranges with a formula. Phase 1 refinement stores the first 11 ranges explicitly, consistent with [VIZ's official volume-grouped chapter list](https://www.viz.com/shonenjump/chapters/chainsaw-man?locale=en) and Shueisha's volume contents. [Volume 1](https://books.shueisha.co.jp/items/contents.html?jdcn=08881780881780315501) and [Volume 24](https://books.shueisha.co.jp/items/contents.html?jdcn=08X10000000071957700) additionally provide verified Japanese print dates, and volume 24's chapter range. Volumes 12–23 have no chapter range or date in the current dataset and display a clear unverified state.

The story arc boundaries in `arcs.ts` come from the supplied prototype and remain approximate. Part 2 volumes link only to the broad Academy Saga overview, rather than claiming a more specific unverified arc association.
